ASSISTANT COACH

Matt Smith

A RECORD-SETTING HORNET BUILDS HIS COACHING CAREER AT HOME

Matt Smith went from one of the most productive hitters in Sacramento State history directly into the Hornet coaching pipeline.

Smith played for Sacramento State from 2016-21 and finished as a three-time All-WAC selection. He set program career records for hits (276), at-bats (858) and RBI (158), and helped the Hornets win the 2019 WAC Tournament and reach an NCAA Regional.

His best offensive season came in 2021, when he hit a career-high .393. He was first-team All-WAC in 2019 and 2021, second-team All-WAC in 2018 and a WAC All-Tournament selection in 2019. Those years gave him a long view of the standards, routines and offensive adjustments required inside the Hornet program.

Smith moved directly into coaching at Sacramento State, progressing from graduate assistant to volunteer assistant and then full-time assistant coach. His current responsibilities include helping develop the outfielders, working with hitters, coaching first base and assisting across the program.

Sacramento State’s official biography does not list a professional playing stop for Smith. His post-playing experience has been coaching-focused, giving the staff a former elite Hornet player who transitioned immediately into day-to-day player development.
