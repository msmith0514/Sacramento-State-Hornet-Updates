ASSOCIATE HEAD COACH · HITTING

David Flores

FROM HORNET INFIELDER TO PRO HITTER TO HITTING COACH

David Flores has experienced player development from nearly every angle: Sacramento State standout, professional hitter, minor-league coach and now associate head coach at his alma mater.

Flores played four seasons for Sacramento State from 2005-08 and earned All-WAC recognition before the Houston Astros selected him in the 18th round of the 2008 MLB Draft. He left Sacramento with school records in at-bats, doubles and hit-by-pitches and ranked among the program leaders in hits, runs and RBI.

His professional career lasted four seasons in the Astros system. Flores reached Double-A Corpus Christi in 2011 and finished his minor-league career with a .258 average, 47 home runs and 192 RBI across 362 games. That playing background gives his hitting instruction a direct connection to the daily demands of professional baseball.

Flores then moved into coaching. He coached infielders at Sacramento State, served as a hitting coach in the Seattle Mariners organization—including the Midwest League and the Mariners Dominican Academy—and had the opportunity to mentor future Major Leaguers including Julio Rodríguez, Noelvi Marte and Luis Rengifo. He returned to Sacramento State in 2019 and was elevated to associate head coach for 2026.

At Sacramento State, his offensive work has included future professional players such as Nathan Lukes, Sam Long, James Outman and Travis Adams, while his recruiting work included All-Americans JP Smith and Wehiwa Aloy.
