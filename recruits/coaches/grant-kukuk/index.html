PITCHING COACH · RECRUITING COORDINATOR

Grant Kukuk

A HORNET PITCHER RETURNS TO BUILD THE NEXT WAVE

Grant Kukuk returned to Sacramento State in 2026 with a coaching résumé built around pitching development, recruiting and measurable staff improvement.

Kukuk pitched for Sacramento State in 2015-16 after two seasons at the College of Southern Idaho. With the Hornets he moved from a relief role into the weekend rotation, finishing his Sacramento State career 7-7 with a 4.67 ERA. As a senior he worked 82.2 innings and ranked 45th nationally at 1.52 walks per nine innings.

His coaching path began quickly after his playing career and has included the College of Southern Idaho, Cal State East Bay, Utah Valley and Pacific. At Utah Valley, his staff set a school record with 480 strikeouts while lowering its ERA by more than a run and cutting walks per nine innings.

Kukuk did not have a professional playing career listed in Sacramento State’s official biography. His professional-baseball connection instead comes through development: pitchers he coached have moved into MLB organizations. At Utah Valley, Casey Anderson was drafted in the 11th round by Arizona and Mitch Mueller signed with Minnesota. His Division I staffs have repeatedly produced pitchers who were drafted or signed professionally.

He has also coached outside the college season with the Yakima Valley Pippins of the West Coast League and the Neptune Beach Pearl in the California Collegiate Summer League, adding another layer of evaluation and player-development experience.
